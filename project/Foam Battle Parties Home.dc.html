Safety First

Big Fun, Played Safe

Protective goggles are worn by every player, every game.
Blasters shoot soft, round foam balls only.
Kids always have adult supervision.
Blasters are never aimed at people who aren't playing, animals or vehicles.
Games are played away from roads and other unsafe areas.
Equipment must not be modified.
Only approved foam ammunition may be used.
Players follow age and manufacturer safety guidelines.
No physical fighting or dangerous contact.
Equipment damage must be reported.
Equipment must be returned on time.
Customers follow all provided operating instructions.
